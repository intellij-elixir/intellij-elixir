Finds all Elixir references that are unresolved or resolve only to invalid results, such as a call at an arity its name is not defined at.

This is for checking for bugs/missing features in IntelliJ Elixir references and resolving. It might find errors in your code, but the compiler can already do that better.

Reports aliases and calls that resolve to nothing, "Does not resolve to anything", or to only invalid results, "Only resolves to invalid results".

A call whose name is defined at other arities names them, as the compiler's "Did you mean" does: "Only resolves to invalid results; defined as snoc/2, snoc/3". A remote call lists at most five.

A remote call to a name its module exports at no arity gets up to five of the module's functions and macros with similar names, listed by name: "Does not resolve to anything. Did you mean: snoc/2?".

Elixir code in documentation, such as an iex> sample, is not compiled and is not checked.
